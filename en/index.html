Why Vertice exists

Those who build audiences shouldn't depend on someone else's platform.

Social networks and the big platforms kept the audience, the algorithm and the revenue of those who produce content. At the same time, vertical drama showed that narrative and product can be the same thing: the hook is the script and the paywall. And AI made it possible for storytellers to run their own machine. Vertice joins both ends, platform technology and content intelligence, to take your public from the social network to your app, under your brand and with your revenue.

Over 70% of new streaming platforms launch on white-label technology. The difference is who runs the monetization.

Artificial intelligence

The "AI" in the name works on every screen.

Not a chatbot bolted onto the app. It's the intelligence that decides what to show, in which language, whom to charge and when to bring them back.

  • Recommendations and feed personalized by behavior, time of day and genre.
  • Automatic dubbing and subtitles in Portuguese, Spanish, English and Italian.
  • Conversational agents in-app and on WhatsApp: support, billing, subscriber win-back.
  • Dynamic pricing and fraud detection for coins, bundles and unlocks.
  • Content intelligence: what to produce, where to cut the episode, where to place the paywall.
  • Assisted creation: scripts, covers, synopses and moderation for operations with user-generated content.
